The AndroidManifest.xml

Forgot to register your Service? Your app crashes. Forgot permissions? It fails silently.

File: AndroidManifest.xml
<manifest xmlns:android="...">
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" />
<application ... >
<activity android:name=".MainActivity">
  <intent-filter> ... </intent-filter>
</activity>
<service android:name=".MyBackgroundService" />
</application>
</manifest>

Service vs. Activity Lifecycle

Services are simpler. Don't overcomplicate them in the exam.

Activity (Complex)

onCreate()
↓
onStart()
↓
onResume()
↓
onPause()
↓
onStop()
↓
onDestroy()

Service (Simple)

onCreate()
Run once (Setup)
↓
onStartCommand()
Runs every time startService() is called
↓
onDestroy()
Cleanup

Pre-Exam Checklist

  • ✅ Did I add <uses-permission> to the Manifest?
  • ✅ Is my Service registered in the <application> tag?
  • ✅ Do I have a separate XML file for my RecyclerView row?
  • ✅ Is my Database logic on a background thread?
